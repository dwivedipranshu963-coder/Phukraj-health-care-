About Phukrajhealthindia

🏆 Established 1991 — 30+ Years Strong

Phukrajhealthindia Pvt. Ltd. was founded in 1991 with a singular mission — to bring the healing power of nature to every Indian household. What began as a small herbal formulation unit in Punjab has grown into one of India's most trusted Ayurvedic wellness brands.

Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facility, Phukraj Organic India, is equipped with modern infrastructure while staying true to traditional Ayurvedic principles. Every product is made from carefully sourced herbs, lab-tested for purity and potency, and formulated by expert Ayurvedic practitioners.

Over three decades, we have helped thousands of families across India achieve better health naturally — without chemicals, without side effects, and with complete trust.
